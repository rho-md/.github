## Rho MD

Read, write and organize what you know, in plain Markdown files. Free for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iPhone
and iPad.

**[Download](https://rho.md/download.html)** · [rho.md](https://rho.md) · [Release history](https://github.com/rho-md/rho-md/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md) · [Report a bug or suggest a feature](https://github.com/rho-md/rho-md/issues/new/choose)

The Rho MD app is closed source; [rho-md/rho-md](https://github.com/rho-md/rho-md) holds its release history and
issue tracker. Our open-source projects: [MarkView](https://github.com/scos-lab/markview) and
[rho-plugins](https://github.com/scos-lab/rho-plugins).
